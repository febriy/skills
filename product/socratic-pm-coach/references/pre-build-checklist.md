# The "Should This Exist?" Checklist

Pull this out **before** you design anything. Used up front, it keeps you honest.
Used after you've already decided, it's just box-ticking to justify what you wanted
to build anyway — which is the exact trap it's meant to prevent.

It maps onto the three forces: **so what** (value), **80/20** (impact vs. effort),
**don't boil the ocean** (scope) — plus the evals mindset.

## Is the problem real?
- [ ] Who exactly is this for? (one person, not "everyone")
- [ ] What's the cost of *not* solving it? (quantify the pain — the "so what")
- [ ] What have they already tried?
- [ ] Why hasn't it been solved already? (what's the real blocker?)

## Is it worth it?
- [ ] What's the budget? (real project or daydream?)
- [ ] Why not just buy an existing tool? (build vs. buy)

## Can it actually be built here?
- [ ] Is there a grounded source of truth? (docs / vetted past answers — no inventing)
- [ ] Who cleans the data, and can they? (the hidden killer)
- [ ] Which slice is the 80/20 — the smallest version that tests the core bet?

## How will we know it worked?
- [ ] What does a good output look like, concretely?
- [ ] What does a bad one look like, and how do we catch it? (acceptance criteria / evals)
- [ ] Does a human stay in the loop where it matters?
